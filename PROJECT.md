## Positioning
**24/7 maintenance + emergency plumber** for residential and commercial. Renovations/new-build experience exists but the brand is pushing into **maintenance specialist** territory — blocked drains, burst pipes, hot water, drainage diagnostics. This is the SEO + GEO sweet spot for a new local domain.

- Blocked drains (sewer, stormwater, toilet, sink)

- Leaking tap & fixture repairs
