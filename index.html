● 05 — Common Questions

Plumbing questions,
straight answers.

How do I unblock a sewer drain?
Most sewer blockages need a licensed plumber with a high-pressure drain jetter and a CCTV camera. We locate the blockage, clear it with the jetter, then run the camera to confirm the line is clean. Don't pour chemical drain cleaners down a fully blocked sewer — they can damage pipes.
What's the difference between a blocked sewer and blocked stormwater?
The sewer carries wastewater from toilets, sinks and washing machines to Sydney Water's network. Stormwater carries rainwater from roofs and yards to council drains. They're separate systems — a blocked sewer causes indoor backups, while blocked stormwater causes pooling water outside.
How much does drain jet blasting cost in Sydney?
In the Inner West, drain jet blasting typically runs from around $390 for a straightforward residential blockage up to $850+ for severe tree-root or commercial work. We quote on-site before any work begins — no callout surprises.
How long does a CCTV drain inspection take?
A standard residential CCTV drain inspection takes 30–60 minutes. We feed a high-resolution camera through the line, record the footage, and you watch the screen with us. You leave with a digital recording you can show to insurers or future buyers.
How do I know if I have a burst pipe?
Signs include a sudden drop in water pressure, damp patches on walls or ceilings, the sound of running water when nothing is on, unexplained spikes in your water bill, or visible pooling. If you suspect a burst pipe, turn off the mains and call us — we respond same-day across Concord.
How much does hot water system replacement cost in Concord?
A like-for-like hot water replacement in the Inner West usually runs $1,800–$3,400 supplied and installed, depending on brand, size and gas vs electric. Continuous-flow gas units sit at the higher end. We quote on-site and supply Rinnai, Rheem, Thermann and Bosch.
Why is my toilet gurgling?
A gurgling toilet usually means a partial blockage or vent issue further down the sewer line. Air is being forced back through the water trap. If left untreated it often progresses to a full backup. Book a CCTV inspection — it'll show you exactly what's causing it.
What should I do if there are tree roots in my drain?
Tree roots are the #1 cause of blocked sewers in Inner West properties with older clay pipes. We clear them with a high-pressure jetter, then CCTV-inspect to find the entry point. For repeat offenders we recommend a pipe reline — a no-dig repair that seals the line.

Yes. Optic Plumbing Solutions is operated by Jere Skorin, a licensed NSW plumber with 15+ years of trade experience. All work is performed to AS/NZS 3500 plumbing standards. Verify any NSW plumber's licence at the NSW Fair Trading register.
